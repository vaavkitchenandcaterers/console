# The 66 labels — as shipped

Every set now carries an occasion label and at least one occasion tag, enforced by `menu-data.test.js`. **These were authored from the dish lists, not from the kitchen — they are proposals awaiting sign-off.** Correct anything that is wrong and the change is one line in `menu-data.js`.

Rules the validator enforces: labels unique, 28 characters or fewer, every set tagged with at least one occasion, and every occasion backed by at least one set. Occasions: `wedding` `reception` `seemantham` `housewarming` `puja` `birthday` `corporate` `temple`.

The **Set** column is the internal name. It does not change — it stays the key the Studio matches on.
