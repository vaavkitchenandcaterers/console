Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you a pure vegetarian caterer?

Yes. VAAV is 100% pure vegetarian — every menu, every event. We don't cook or serve any non-vegetarian food.

Which areas do you serve?

Our kitchen is in Perungalathur (Peerkankaranai) and we cater across all of Chennai. We also take outstation events — message us with your location and date and we'll confirm.

What's the minimum order?

From 30 guests for a tiffin spread and 50 for a full virundhu sappadu — up to 2,000+ plates for large weddings.

How much does catering cost?

Catering is priced per plate and shaped around your menu, guest count and event. Message us on WhatsApp for a quick quote.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as you can, especially for weekend weddings and festival dates. Message us with your date and we'll confirm availability straight away.

How do I book and pay?

We take a 50% advance to confirm your booking, with the balance due on or before the event day. Message or call +91 96553 56333 and we'll lock in your date.

Do you serve on banana leaf?

Yes, the traditional way — with buffet or table service also available on request.

Can the menu be customised (Jain / no onion-garlic)?

Yes, our menus are fully customisable, including sattvic onion- and garlic-free menus for pujas.

Do you provide cooks and servers?

Yes — our packages include cooks and servers, so you can just enjoy the day.

Do you offer a tasting before booking?

Yes — we're happy to arrange a tasting so you can plan your menu with confidence. Just ask us when you enquire.

Do you cater non-vegetarian food?

No — we are proudly 100% pure vegetarian.

What are your hours?

Monday to Sunday, 7 AM to 9 PM. Message or call +91 96553 56333 anytime in that window.

Do you do office lunches or hostel contracts?

Yes — daily office lunches, hostel and mess contracts, and one-off bulk orders, all GST-invoiced. See how corporate catering works or message us with your headcount.
