Our Story

Rooted in Tamil Tradition, Crafted for Every Occasion

Vaav Kitchen and Caterings is a Chennai-based catering service specialising in authentic South Indian Tamil cuisine. Based in Perungalathur, we serve families and businesses across Chennai with home-style recipes passed down through generations — from the classic banana leaf sadhya to modern buffet spreads.

Every dish is prepared with fresh ingredients, traditional cooking methods, and genuine care — whether it's a wedding for 1000 guests or an intimate house function for 20.

  • ✔ Hygienic preparation & on-time delivery
  • ✔ Customisable menus for every budget
  • ✔ Experienced cooks & service staff
  • ✔ Veg & Non-Veg Tamil specialities
